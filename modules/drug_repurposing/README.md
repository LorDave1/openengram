# drug_repurposing

**Status:** 💡 open for contributors. Nothing implemented yet.

## Question
Rank existing approved drugs by their connection to dementia-related genes and pathways in biomedical knowledge graphs. Output is research hypotheses only, never medical advice.

## Possible data
PrimeKG, Hetionet (CC0).

## Want to lead this?
Open an issue titled "[drug_repurposing] proposal" with your plan, the data you'd use and its license.
Follow the rules in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) and [SAFETY.md](../../SAFETY.md).
